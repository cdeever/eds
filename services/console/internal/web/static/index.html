      Talking to EdS

      Say Hey DJ Ed, then ask. The music turns down while you are heard and comes back after. EdS knows only the requests below; anything else is answered as not understood, never with a guess.

      An album

      • Play Abbey Road by the Beatles
      • Play Abbey Road from the Beatles
      • Play the Beatles album Abbey Road
      • Play the Beatles, Abbey Road
      • Play Abbey Road: alone, when only one artist you have made it

      An album by its place

      • Play Van Halen's first album
      • Play the third album by Boston
      • Play the latest album by AC/DC

      First to tenth, debut, latest, newest or most recent. Counted among the artist's studio albums; one you have not got is skipped for the next you have.

      A song

      • Play the song Come Together by the Beatles
      • Play the Beatles song Come Together
      • Play Come Together by the Beatles: when it is not one of their albums you have

      Always with the artist.

      An artist, shuffled

      • Play some Van Halen
      • Play songs by Genesis
      • Shuffle Aerosmith
      • Play the Beatles: alone, when you have music by them

      Ways to ask

      Put on Rocks by Aerosmith, Let's hear Rocks by Aerosmith, I want to hear Rocks by Aerosmith, Can I hear Rocks by Aerosmith: all the same as play. Please anywhere is fine; don't or not before it means no.

      What plays comes from the iTunes library, by its own names.
